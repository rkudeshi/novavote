NoVA November General Election DAL metrics archive

Included standardized years: 2023 and 2024.
Jurisdictions included each year:
- Alexandria City
- Arlington County
- Fairfax City
- Fairfax County
- Falls Church City
- Loudoun County
- Manassas City
- Manassas Park City
- Prince William County

Source: Electoral Process Education Corp. / Digital Poll Watchers, derived from Virginia Department of Elections Daily Absentee List (DAL) snapshots.

These files are locality-level cumulative daily DAL metrics, not voter-level DAL files and not early-voting-site totals. ON_MACHINE is the cumulative approved in-person early-vote metric. MAIL_IN is the cumulative MARKED + PRE_PROCESSED metric.

2022: Digital Poll Watchers documented daily locality/precinct CSV publication, but the currently linked distribution archive is broken/unavailable, so 2022 is not included here rather than substituting an incompatible dataset.
2021 and 2020: EPEC has/had DAL-derived and compiled historical data, but not in this standardized byLOCALITY_NAME daily metrics export format currently exposed on the site, so they are not included in this package.
